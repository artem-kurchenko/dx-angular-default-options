Approach 2 — wrappers that accept projected configuration

Each wrapper carries the house style and exposes two customisation channels: projected dxo-* configuration components, and plain @Input()s. Projected configuration overrides the house style; anything not projected keeps it.

Why columns come in as data and are rendered by the card

dxi-* collection items are gathered by the inner component's own content query, and Angular queries match by declaration view — so a column written inside <app-dx-grid-card> is a content child of the card, never of the grid inside it, and <ng-content> does not change that. Forwarding them by hand makes matters worse: the inner query then resolves empty and overwrites the forwarded list, leaving a grid with no columns and no error.

So the card takes columns as data and renders real <dxi-data-grid-column> components in its own template. Declared there they are content children of the grid, its query finds them normally, and they behave exactly as hand-written markup would — note that orderDate still gets its caption generated automatically.
